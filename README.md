4. Skipping Property Inspections

Understanding the legalities surrounding property ownership is crucial. Always verify ownership documents, title deeds, and any ongoing litigation related to the property.

It's easy to feel pressured when faced with a competitive market, but rushing your decision can lead to regret. Take your time to evaluate your options and consult experts if needed.
